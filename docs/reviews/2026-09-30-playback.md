# Review: battery drain and screen-off connection alarms

Reviewed release `v1.1.0` (`cbaf776`) against its previous release base
`d36c359`. Reported behavior: greater streamer battery drain and connection-loss
alarms after the receiver's screen turns off, probably while in audio-only mode.
No Android device was connected during this review.

## Confirmed code issues and local fixes

1. **Invisible video remains active.** The new independent video player is
   controlled by the listening preference and connection state, but not by
   activity visibility or screen state. Removing the playback surface does not
   stop its RTSP session. Consequently the sender can keep its camera and encoder
   active after the receiver is locked, and video retries remain possible while
   no picture is visible. This is a confirmed power-management gap; it does not
   explain a session that was already explicitly audio-only.

   Fixed: activity visibility and service-owned screen broadcasts now gate the
   video session and retries. Audio stays connected. The saved video preference
   is preserved, so returning to visible playback restores video automatically.
   Visible picture-in-picture playback remains eligible for video.

2. **Camera failures can interrupt healthy audio.** Sender camera errors and
   video encoder errors previously used the same shutdown path as audio/server
   errors. With independently managed video sessions, a video-only failure could
   stop the microphone and RTSP server, provoking receiver recovery and an alarm
   if audio did not recover within the configured interval.

   Fixed: asynchronous camera/video encoder failures release video resources and
   report video unavailable while preserving running audio and the RTSP server.
   Actual microphone/server failures still use the fatal recovery path. This is
   a confirmed failure path, not evidence that it caused the reported incidents.

3. **Streamer status reports capability instead of capture activity.** Both the
   screen and notification could say video was live while the camera was asleep.

   Fixed: status reflects active capture, camera sleeping with audio live, or
   video unavailable with audio live.

## Audio-only alarm: still needs device evidence

The audio player still uses `WAKE_MODE_NETWORK` in a foreground media service.
Those protections were not removed in 1.1.0. The 15-second interval is the
configured outage-alarm delay; it does not identify why audio became unavailable.
The review did not reproduce the reported screen-off failure or establish an
Android battery restriction as its cause. No alarm threshold was extended and
real screen-off audio failures continue to arm the alarm.

The 1.1.0 microphone changes had switched capture to communication mode and
enabled supported echo/noise effects continuously. The current local changes
remove those effects and use the standard microphone source again, as requested.
The battery cost of the released behavior was not measured on the actual phone,
so its contribution to the reported increase remains unknown.

Added `BabyCamReceiver` diagnostic events for screen state, audio connection
attempts, audio errors (numeric code and cause class), recovery, and alarm timing.
These omit stream URLs, credentials, and media content.

## Verification and remaining device checks

Automated coverage includes screen-off video suspension without audio changes,
restoration on visible playback, audio-only and paused behavior across screen
cycles, genuine audio-outage alarms at 15 seconds while the screen is off,
multiple video viewers, camera failure isolation, and actual-capture status.

Hardware follow-up:

- Keep an audio-only receiver locked for several minutes and record
  `adb logcat -v threadtime -s BabyCamReceiver` during an alarm.
- Compare the sender's active camera clients and codec resources before and
  after receiver screen-off; test that another video viewer keeps capture active.
- Compare unplugged battery use under the same resolution, network, and duration.

These are local working-tree fixes, not a published or installed update.
